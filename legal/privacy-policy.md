# Dovvy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Dovvy is operated by Diligentic Infotech Canada Inc. ("we", "us"). Dovvy turns business cards into contact records and helps you prepare follow-up emails. This policy explains what we collect, how we use and share it, and your choices.

Privacy Officer: Ajay Kumar, ajay@diligentic.com

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Name, username, email address, password (stored as a hash), profile settings, and session information such as IP address and browser type.

**Business card and contact information.** Card images you capture or upload, text and QR data extracted from them, names, job titles, companies, email addresses, phone numbers, websites, addresses, links, notes you write, and AI-generated company and person summaries.

**Outreach information.** The instructions you write and the contacts you select to generate email drafts, the drafts generated, and how many drafts you have created.

**Billing information.** Subscription status, usage counts, and invoice references. Stripe collects and processes your payment card details. We do not store full card numbers.

**Support enquiries.** Your name, email, topic, and message.

## 2. How we use information

We use it to provide the service you ask for: reading cards, saving and exporting contacts, researching contacts, writing drafts, syncing with Google, managing your account and subscription, sending account emails, providing support, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Card images and AI processing

Card images are processed to extract their contents. We do not store card images on our servers after processing.

We use Google's Gemini API to read cards, summarize company websites, generate company and person summaries and suggested profile links, and write drafts. The content needed for each task is sent to Google. If a contact has a website, our server may retrieve its public text to identify the services listed.

AI output can be wrong or incomplete. Review it before you rely on it.

## 4. Google user data

Connecting Google is optional apart from the sign-in method you choose. We request only these permissions:

| Permission | What Dovvy does with it |
|---|---|
| openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile | Signs you in and identifies your account using your name, email address, profile information, and Google account identifier. |
| drive.file | Creates a spreadsheet for your contacts, or works with a file you have made available to Dovvy. Dovvy reads rows to display contacts, check for duplicates and maintain headers, and writes contact rows and formatting. It cannot see your other Drive files. |

Dovvy does not request any Gmail permission. It cannot read, create, or send email in your account. When you choose to open a draft in Gmail or your mail app, the recipient, subject, and message are passed to that app through your browser.

**Storage and protection.** We store Google authorization tokens, the permissions you granted, and the ID, address, and name of your linked spreadsheet. Data is encrypted in transit. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest.

**Sharing.** We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide the features you use (including our hosting providers), to comply with law, for security purposes, or as part of a merger or sale with your consent.

**Limited Use.** Dovvy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, we do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use Google Workspace data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models. People do not read it unless you give permission for specific data, it is necessary for security or to comply with law, or it is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

**Removing access.** You can revoke Dovvy at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored tokens and related data deleted, email ajay@diligentic.com. Spreadsheets already in your Google account stay there until you delete them.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and contact data while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days. Copies in backups and logs are removed as they expire on a rolling schedule. We may keep limited billing and legal records for as long as the law requires.

To request deletion, email ajay@diligentic.com from your account address. Cancelling a paid plan does not delete your account.

## 7. Security

We use safeguards such as encryption in transit, password hashing, and access controls. No system is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and regulators as the law requires.

## 8. Browser storage

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, complete Google sign-in, and remember preferences. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

## 9. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent. Email ajay@diligentic.com. We respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## 10. People whose cards are scanned

If a Dovvy user saved your details and you want them accessed, corrected, or deleted, email ajay@diligentic.com. Users are responsible for having a lawful reason to collect and use their contacts' information.

## 11. Children

Dovvy is for people aged 18 or older.

## 12. Changes

We will post updates here and change the effective date. We will notify you of significant changes, and get consent where required, including before using Google user data in a new way.
